**1st level**
- *Hunter's Mark* — bonus action, concentration, +1d6 damage to the target.
- *Cure Wounds* — touch, heals 1d8 + 2.
- *Goodberry* — ten berries, each heals 1 HP.
- *Fog Cloud* — 20 ft. sphere of fog, concentration.

**2nd level**
- *Pass without Trace* — +10 to Stealth for the party, concentration.
- *Spike Growth* — 20 ft. of hidden thorns, 2d4 per 5 ft. moved.

**Gloom Stalker spells**
- *Disguise Self* — always prepared.
- *Rope Trick* — always prepared.
